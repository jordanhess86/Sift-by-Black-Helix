    Welcome to Sift

    Three quick steps to get everything ready. You can do them any time.

    1. Choose a backup folder

      Backups and search data go here. Pick a folder outside iCloud, Dropbox and similar services, so your chats stay on this computer.

    2. Back up your chats

      Saves up to 200 chats per run, so large accounts may need a few runs. Search can only find chats that are backed up.

    3. Prepare related search

      Lets search find chats by topic as well as exact words. This runs on your computer, and nothing is sent anywhere.
